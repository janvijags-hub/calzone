# Calzone product demo: narration script (about 2 minutes)

Written for a 6th-grade listener: short sentences, everyday words, every new word explained.
AI voice (ElevenLabs), labelled "AI voice" on screen. No student names, faces or voices. No NASA logo.
Every number is from the app's own screens (Nov 15, 2026 test with openly licensed plate photos) or from
the cited sources (NASA studies on PubMed; NASA JSC-67378; FDA; USDA).

| id | picture | seconds | narration |
|---|---|---|---|
| s01 | Title slide | 8 | This is Calzone. It is part of our HelloVitals app. Take a picture of your food, and Calzone tells you what is in it. |
| s02 | Problem slide | 16 | Keeping track of what you eat is hard. In space, it is even harder. NASA found that astronauts on the space station ate too little food. They also need more vitamin D, because there is no sunlight in space. And they have to be careful with iron. |
| s03 | Phone: Calzone tab, Upload photos, picker | 10 | On the iPhone, open the Calzone tab. You can use smart glasses, take a photo, or upload a picture you already have. |
| s04 | Phone: scan result | 9 | A computer program called an A.I. looks at the plate. In about six seconds, it lists each food and guesses how much there is. |
| s05 | Phone: food list, fixing a match | 12 | But the A.I. can make mistakes. Here it said chicken curry. It was really a cheese curry. So you check each food, and pick the right one from a list. |
| s06 | Phone: nutrient bars | 14 | Now for the numbers. They come from a big government food list, not from the A.I. Each nutrient has two bars. One shows what you need on Earth. The other shows what you need in space. |
| s07 | Phone: iron row | 10 | Look at iron. On Earth, this plate gives about a quarter of what you need in a day. In space, it is already half of the limit. |
| s08 | Phone: today's total | 7 | Add the plate to your day. Calzone adds up everything you eat, on the phone and on the computer. |
| s09 | Mac page | 10 | Calzone also works on a computer web page. You can upload pictures, and find real science papers about each vitamin. |
| s10 | How it works slide | 9 | Everything runs on our own computer, so it works without the internet, just like on a spaceship. And your pictures are not saved. |
| s11 | Closing slide | 10 | We used A.I. to help build Calzone, and this voice is made by A.I. too. Calzone: look at your plate, and know what is in it. |
